GhostMe

Move your iPhone's GPS from your Mac, over USB.

Download for macOS

macOS 11+ · requires a USB-connected iPhone on iOS 17+

Install

  1. Open the downloaded GhostMe.dmg and drag GhostMe into Applications.
  2. Right-click (Control-click) GhostMe in Applications and choose Open.
  3. Click Open in the dialog that appears. You only need to do this once.

GhostMe isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS will flag it as from an "unidentified developer" the first time — that's what the right-click step above is for. If macOS still refuses to open it, run xattr -cr /Applications/GhostMe.app in Terminal.
